## Find the Repeatable Work

Small teams feel every hour lost to routine. List the tasks that recur each week and follow the same pattern, such as reports, reconciliations, and onboarding checklists. Agents deliver value there first because the rules already exist.

## Design for Handoffs

Most delays live between people rather than inside tasks. Let agents own the handoff by collecting inputs, preparing the next step, and notifying the right owner, so work keeps moving even when everyone is busy.

## Keep Outputs Consistent

Templates, checklists, and fixed formats let an agent deliver the same quality on the hundredth run as on the first. Consistency is what turns an impressive demo into a process the rest of the company can build on.

## Review and Improve

Hold a short monthly review of what each agent did, where it hesitated, and what people still fixed by hand. Small changes to instructions and permissions add up quickly, and the playbook improves with every cycle.
