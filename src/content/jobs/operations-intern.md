## About this role

This internship supports the team behind onboarding, documentation, and day-to-day operations. You will help customers get value quickly, keep internal processes tidy, and try our own agents on the work that keeps a small company running.

## Responsibilities

- Help coordinate customer onboarding sessions
- Keep documentation and help articles up to date
- Track operational metrics in shared dashboards
- Organize feedback for the product team
- Automate recurring internal tasks with our agents

## Requirements

- Strong written communication and organization
- Comfort with spreadsheets and everyday SaaS tools
- Reliability when juggling several small projects

## Nice to Have

- Interest in automation and AI products
- Previous experience in a support or operations role
- A habit of spotting and fixing broken processes
