## About this role

You will improve how agents reason, choose tools, and recover from mistakes. That means building evaluation suites, running experiments on prompts and models, and turning the results into measurable gains in accuracy and cost.

## Responsibilities

- Design evaluations that reflect real agent tasks
- Experiment with prompting, planning, and tool selection
- Reduce failure rates with better recovery strategies
- Balance quality, latency, and cost across models
- Document findings and ship improvements to production

## Requirements

- Practical experience applying machine learning in production
- Strong Python skills and sound experimental habits
- Clear communication of trade-offs to non-specialists

## Nice to Have

- Experience with retrieval, fine-tuning, or model routing
- Contributions to open evaluation or agent tooling
- Exposure to safety and guardrail techniques
