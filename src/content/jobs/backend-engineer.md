## About this role

You will design and run the services that let agents plan, call tools, and report results reliably. The work spans APIs, queues, and data stores, with a strong focus on correctness, observability, and low latency as usage grows.

## Responsibilities

- Design and maintain APIs that power agent workflows
- Build integrations with external tools and data sources
- Improve reliability through retries, idempotency, and monitoring
- Tune databases and background jobs for scale
- Partner with product and ML to ship new capabilities

## Requirements

- Several years building production backend services
- Confidence with distributed systems and asynchronous processing
- Good judgment on security, performance, and data integrity

## Nice to Have

- Hands-on experience with LLM tooling or agent frameworks
- Background with tracing, metrics, and alerting stacks
- Time spent at an early-stage product company
